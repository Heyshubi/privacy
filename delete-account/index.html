Delete your KEYBO account

KEYBO keyboard — Android (com.aikeyboard.app), iPhone and the browser extension
Last updated: 31 October 2026

In the app

Open KEYBO, then Settings › Account › Delete account, and confirm. The account is deleted at once and you are logged out on every device.

Without the app

Email hellokeybo@gmail.com from the address you log in with, with the subject “Delete my KEYBO account”. If you use Sign in with Apple with Hide My Email, write from any address and give us the relay address shown in your Apple ID settings. We delete the account within 30 days and reply when it is done.

What is deleted

  • the account and its email address;
  • how you log in (Google, Apple or email) and that provider's number for you;
  • the devices logged in to it, and their sessions;
  • the plan linked to the account, and its AI usage counts;
  • any login codes still waiting.

What is not

  • A subscription is not cancelled. Google Play or the App Store bills it, so cancel it there. Until it ends, its Premium stays with the phone it was bought on.
  • The plan and usage counts our server keeps for each install, against a random installation number rather than you, as the privacy policy describes. To have those deleted too, say so in the same email.
  • Daily totals — how many installs were active, how many answers were given — which are counts with nothing in them about you.

Nothing you type is ever stored, so there are no messages to delete.

AI Keyboard — Privacy Policy

Application ID: com.aikeyboard.app
Last updated: 30 August 2026

The short version. What you type is not collected, not stored and not sent anywhere. Text leaves your phone only at the moment you tap one of the AI buttons, and only the message you are working on. It is used to produce the rewrite, returned to you, and not kept.

Who this is from

AI Keyboard is made by Shubham (Hey Shubi). If you have a question about anything here, or want data deleted, write to heyshubigraphics@gmail.com.

What the keyboard can see, and what it does with it

A keyboard has to receive what you type in order to type it. Android shows a warning about this when you enable any keyboard, including this one. What matters is what the keyboard then does, and this one does nothing: your keystrokes are used to insert characters into the app you are using and are not recorded, analysed, stored or transmitted.

There is no logging of what you type. There is no learning dictionary, no typing history and no cloud sync.

When text leaves your phone

Only when you tap one of the AI buttons on the toolbar — Fix, Rewrite, Friendly, Professional, Flirty, Hinglish, Translate, or a tone you created yourself. Tapping one sends the text currently in the box to our server so it can be rewritten, and the rewritten text comes back and replaces it.

Nothing is sent while you are simply typing. No button, no transmission.

What is sent

The message text Only the contents of the text box you are in, at the moment you tap the button.
Which button you tapped For example rewrite or translate, plus the language you chose, or the wording of a tone you wrote yourself.
A random installation number Generated on this device the first time the app runs. It is a random number and nothing else — see below.

Where AI buttons never work

The AI buttons are removed, not merely disabled, in:

  • password fields of every kind — text passwords, web passwords, the “show password” variety, and numeric PINs;
  • any field an app has marked as private, which is how a browser marks an incognito tab and how banking and health apps mark sensitive fields.

In those boxes the toolbar says so and the buttons are gone. There is no setting to switch this off. Typing works as normal.

The random installation number

The app generates a random number the first time it runs and sends it with each rewrite. It exists for one reason: to count how many rewrites this installation has used today, so the free daily allowance can be applied.

It is not your advertising ID, your phone's serial number, your account, or anything else that identifies you or your device. It is not linked to any other information. Uninstalling the app discards it; reinstalling generates a new one. The server keeps only a count against it, in memory, and that count is discarded when the counter resets or the server restarts.

Who processes the text

The rewrite itself is performed by Anthropic's Claude model. Our server passes your message to Anthropic's API, receives the rewritten text, and returns it to your phone. Anthropic processes it in order to produce the reply. See Anthropic's privacy policy for how they handle API data.

Our server is hosted on Railway, which operates the infrastructure it runs on. Neither our server nor the app writes your message to a database, a file or a log.

The text is not sold, rented, or shared with anyone else. It is not used for advertising, and it is not used to build a profile of you.

What stays on your phone

Your settings are stored on the device and never sent anywhere: the theme, the see-through level, the keyboard size, your default tone, the tones you wrote yourself, the language Translate opens on, the emoji you used recently, and the number of rewrites left today.

If you have Android Backup switched on, Google may include these settings in your device backup, in the same way it does for other apps. That backup is between you and Google; we do not receive it.

Permissions

The app requests exactly one Android permission: INTERNET, which it needs to reach the rewrite server. It does not request contacts, storage, location, camera, microphone, or the phone state.

Analytics, advertising and tracking

There are none. The app contains no analytics library, no advertising library, no crash reporter and no third-party tracking of any kind. The only libraries it includes are Google's own AndroidX and Material interface components, which do not collect data.

Children

AI Keyboard is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, there is nothing held about a child to delete — but if you believe otherwise, write to the address above and we will look into it.

Your choices and your rights

  • Stop text being sent at all — do not tap the AI buttons. The keyboard works fully without them.
  • Remove everything — uninstalling the app deletes every setting it stored and discards the random installation number. There is no account to close, because there is no account.
  • Ask a question, or ask for deletion — write to heyshubigraphics@gmail.com. Since messages are not stored, there is normally nothing to delete, but we will confirm that to you.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page changes with it, and the current version is always the one published at this address. Material changes will also be described in the app's release notes on Google Play.

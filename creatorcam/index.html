CreatorCam — Privacy Policy

App: CreatorCam (com.shubhamsaini.creatorcam) for iPhone
Last updated: 9 October 2026

The short version. Everything CreatorCam does happens on your iPhone. Your videos, photos, sound, face and captions are not collected, not uploaded and not sent anywhere. CreatorCam has no account, no server, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. Your recordings leave your iPhone only when you share them yourself.

Who this is from

CreatorCam is made by Shubham Saini. If you have a question about anything here, write to hellokeybo@gmail.com.

What we collect

Nothing. CreatorCam does not connect to any server of ours or of anyone else, and it has no analytics, advertising or tracking code. We cannot see your recordings, and we do not know who uses the app.

If you have chosen in iOS to share analytics with app developers (Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements), Apple may pass us anonymous crash reports for CreatorCam. They describe where the app went wrong and contain none of your recordings. You can switch this off there at any time.

What CreatorCam asks for, and why

PermissionWhat it is used for
CameraTo record videos and take photos.
MicrophoneTo record sound with your videos.
PhotosTo save your recordings into the CreatorCam album. CreatorCam also uses this access to check whether a video it saved is still in Photos, and to mark your “good takes” as Favourites. It does not look at your other photos.
Speech RecognitionOnly if you make captions: Apple's speech recognition turns the speech in your video into text, set to run on your iPhone. The first time you use a language, iOS may download Apple's language model; that download comes from Apple and contains nothing of yours.

The level guide reads your iPhone's motion sensors while it is shown; this needs no permission and is not recorded. You can change any permission in Settings → Apps → CreatorCam (on iOS 17: Settings → CreatorCam).

Faces and beauty

To apply beauty, makeup and face refinement only where they belong, CreatorCam finds faces and visible skin in each camera frame, on your iPhone, using Apple's Vision framework and machine-learning models built into the app. This information is used for that frame only, to draw the effect, and is then discarded. CreatorCam does not recognise who anyone is, does not create or keep face data, and never sends any of it anywhere.

Where your files are kept

  • In the app: each take is kept in CreatorCam's own folder on your iPhone (you can also see it in the Files app under On My iPhone → CreatorCam), together with any captions you made and any LUT files you imported.
  • In Photos: if you allow it, your videos and photos are also saved to the CreatorCam album.
  • Backups: like every app's files, CreatorCam's folder is part of your iPhone's own backup (iCloud Backup or your computer) if you have backups switched on, and the CreatorCam album syncs with iCloud Photos if you use it. Those are handled by Apple under your Apple account, not by us.

You can delete takes in the app at any time. Deleting the app removes its folder; videos you saved to Photos stay in Photos until you delete them there.

Sharing

When you share a video with the share button, it goes only where you send it, and the app or service you choose then handles it under its own policy.

Children

CreatorCam is not directed at children under 13. It collects no personal information from anyone, of any age.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date at the top. If CreatorCam ever starts to collect anything, the policy will say so before that version of the app is released.

Verto Support

Verto — the camera for creators, for iPhone

Need help? Write to hellokeybo@gmail.com. The quickest way is Settings → About → Support in the app: it starts the email with your app version, iOS version and iPhone model filled in. Tell us what you did and what happened. We usually answer within two working days.

What you need

  • An iPhone with iOS 17 or later.
  • Front + Back needs an iPhone XS, XR or newer (iPhones that can run two cameras at once). On other iPhones the option is hidden.
  • HDR video, 4K at 60 fps, ProRAW photos and the Camera Control button appear only on iPhones that support them.

Recording horizontal and vertical at once

  1. Tap the Dual Orientation button (two frames, one tall and one wide) at the top of the camera.
  2. The yellow box shows the second video. Drag it to reframe — also while recording; double-tap to centre it again.
  3. Each take saves two videos, one horizontal and one vertical, with the same sound and in sync.

The sizes each video gets on your iPhone are shown in Settings → Dual Orientation.

Front and back cameras together

  • Tap the Front + Back button (two people) at the top of the camera.
  • Choose picture in picture (a small window you can drag, pinch to resize, and tap to swap the cameras) or split screen (one camera above the other) with the layout button next to it.
  • In Settings → Front + Back choose what a take saves: one merged video, the two cameras as separate videos, or both.
  • Front + Back and H + V can't be on together: that would record up to four videos at once and heat the iPhone quickly.

Beauty and filters

  • Tap the face button to open Beauty. Start from a preset (Natural, Soft Glow, Clean, Fresh, Evening), then fine-tune skin, face, makeup and filters. Reset takes everything back to the start.
  • Press and hold the picture to see it without beauty.
  • Filters include 149 Pro cinematic looks. You can also import your own LUTs (.cube files) from the Files app.
  • To keep an untouched copy of each take as well, turn on Settings → Also Save Original.

Captions

Open a take in the gallery and tap Captions (or Make Captions in the video's ⋯ menu). Captions are made on your iPhone, as an SRT file or drawn onto the video. Languages include Hinglish (Hindi and English in English letters), Hindi, and English — whichever your iPhone can recognise on the device. The first time you use a language, iOS may need to download it.

Sound

  • The microphone button at the top shows the live level; it turns red if the sound is too loud.
  • From the same button: pick a microphone (USB-C, Lightning and Bluetooth microphones, including AirPods, appear when connected), open Mic Mode (Voice Isolation or Wide Spectrum, set by iOS), and set Noise Reduction.

Where are my videos?

  • In the app's gallery (the button at the bottom left of the camera).
  • In the Verto album in Photos, if you allowed Photos access.
  • In the Files app under On My iPhone → Verto.

To make room, Settings → Storage → Free Up Space removes the app's copies of videos that are already in Photos. The videos stay in Photos.

If something goes wrong

The iPhone gets warm

4K at 60 fps with beauty on uses the most power. For long takes choose 4K at 30 fps or 1080p at 60 fps, and avoid recording while charging. When the iPhone gets hot, Verto lowers the effort step by step and tells you, instead of stuttering.

A call comes in, or I left the app

Verto stops the take and saves what was recorded. You'll find it in the gallery.

The camera, microphone or Photos doesn't work

Check the permissions in Settings → Apps → Verto (on iOS 17: Settings → Verto) and turn on Camera, Microphone and Photos.

Privacy

Everything Verto does happens on your iPhone; nothing is uploaded. Read the privacy policy.

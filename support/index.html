Setting up the keyboard

  1. Open Settings → General → Keyboard → Keyboards → Add New Keyboard and choose KEYBO.
  2. Tap KEYBO in that list and turn on Allow Full Access if you want the AI buttons. Typing works without it.
  3. In any app, tap the globe to switch to KEYBO. On an iPad, or an iPhone with a Home button, the globe is on KEYBO's own keys; hold it to see all your keyboards.

The AI buttons are greyed out

They need Full Access (step 2 above) and an internet connection. They also switch off in password fields, on purpose. On the free plan you have 10 AI actions a day; the count starts again at midnight India time.

KEYBO Premium

  • Subscribe in the KEYBO app → Go Premium.
  • Restore on a new device or after reinstalling: KEYBO app → Go Premium → Restore Purchases.
  • Cancel or change: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → KEYBO, or KEYBO app → Premium → Manage subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before it renews.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com.
